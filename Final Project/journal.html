Extremely Important Tree Facts

Please read these facts carefully. There may or may not be a quiz.

Fact 01: Trees are tall

Many trees grow taller than humans. This helps them reach sunlight.

Fact 02: Trees provide shade

On a sunny day, a tree can make a spot more comfortable. Just stand under it.

Fact 03: Trees need sunlight

Trees use sunlight to help make their own food through photosynthesis. No lunch break required.

Fact 04: Trees live in cities too

Urban trees provide shade and can make concrete-heavy places feel greener.

Research Conclusion

Trees are important to ecosystems and people. Please appreciate them responsibly.
